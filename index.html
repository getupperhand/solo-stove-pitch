02 · The idea

Back­yard Sessions

A concert series in real backyards. Emerging artists play a short set by the fire. Guests cook on the Pi, pull drinks from the Windchill, and sit around the Bonfire. One host ties every night together. We film all of it.

5:00

Load in. Light the fire. Crew sets three cameras and hides them.

6:00

Thirty guests arrive. Pizzas go in. The cooler opens.

7:00

7:45

Second artist. Golden hour. This is the hero film.

8:30

Song circle at the fire. S'mores. The part people post.

9:30

Embers. Guests leave with a song stuck in their head.
